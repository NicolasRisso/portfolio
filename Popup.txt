<h2 class="font-h2 text-h2 text-white mb-4 flex items-center gap-3">
<span class="material-symbols-outlined text-secondary" data-icon="terminal">terminal</span>
                                Technical Overview
                            </h2>
<div class="space-y-4 text-on-surface-variant font-body-lg leading-relaxed">
<p>
                                    Neon Dawn is a high-performance custom engine project built from the ground up to explore advanced 
                                    rendering techniques and low-level hardware orchestration. The architecture emphasizes a 
                                    <span class="text-secondary font-semibold">Data-Oriented Design (DOD)</span> approach using a 
                                    proprietary ECS framework to maximize CPU cache efficiency and multi-core utilization.
                                </p>
<p>
                                    The rendering pipeline utilizes a <span class="text-white">Clustered Forward+</span> architecture 
                                    implemented via Vulkan 1.3, supporting thousands of dynamic light sources with minimal overhead. 
                                    I developed a custom HLSL-to-SPIR-V transpilation toolchain to streamline shader development and 
                                    enable complex post-processing effects including screen-space reflections and temporal anti-aliasing.
                                </p>
<p>
                                    Optimization was a core pillar of development. Through rigorous profiling with NSight and Pix, 
                                    I implemented a sub-allocator for GPU memory management and a sophisticated occlusion culling 
                                    system that reduced draw calls by 64% in dense urban environments.
                                </p>
</div>
</section>
